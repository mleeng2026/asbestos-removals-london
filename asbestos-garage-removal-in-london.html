ROOF ONLY OR FULL GARAGE?

Keep the asbestos garage removal scope clear before the quote.

“Asbestos garage removal” can mean two different jobs. Some buyers only need asbestos garage roof removal so a roofer can replace the covering. Others want the asbestos cement roof and wall sheets removed and the remaining garage frame dismantled as part of a complete garage removal scope.

Tell us which outcome you want. If the garage is attached to another building, shares a boundary, has restricted access, or sits behind the house with no direct vehicle route, mention that at the start. Access can affect how the materials, wrapped waste and remaining garage components are moved.

If the survey or photos show another asbestos material inside the garage, tell us before the work is priced. AIB, pipe lagging or other higher-risk materials are not the same job as intact asbestos cement sheets and can require a different removal route.

Asbestos Garage Removal London questions.

Do you remove the complete asbestos garage or only the roof?

Both options are available. Call us and say whether you want the asbestos roof removed or the whole asbestos garage dismantled.

Does asbestos cement garage removal need an HSE licence?

Intact asbestos cement can often be removed as non-licensed work by trained workers using safe controls. Damaged material, other asbestos products or a more disruptive method can change the work category, so the material and condition must be checked first.

Can you remove single and double asbestos garages in London?

Yes. We take enquiries for single, double, attached and larger prefabricated garages across all 32 London boroughs.

Can a fire-damaged garage contain asbestos?

It can if asbestos cement roof sheets or wall panels were present. Do not disturb fire-damaged debris. A competent assessment is needed because heat and breakage may have changed the material condition.

What remains after complete asbestos garage removal?

The agreed scope should state whether only asbestos materials are removed or whether the frame and other garage components are also dismantled. The base, foundations and any reinstatement work should be agreed separately.

Can I call for an asbestos garage quote?

Yes. Call us and say whether you want the roof only or the complete garage removed. We will ask for anything else needed to price the work.

Will the asbestos garage waste be disposed of legally?

The removal scope should include suitable wrapping, lawful transport and disposal at an authorised facility, with the relevant waste documentation provided.
