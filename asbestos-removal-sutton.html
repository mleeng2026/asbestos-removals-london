Asbestos Artex removal before rewiring or replastering

Need asbestos Artex removal or asbestos textured coating removal in Sutton? Tell us which ceilings or walls are involved, whether testing has confirmed asbestos, and whether electricians need access above the ceiling. A living-room ceiling, a stairwell and several occupied rooms create different arrangements for access and keeping other people away from the work.

A photograph helps show the surface and room layout; it cannot confirm asbestos content. If you have a laboratory result, include the sampled location so we can relate it to the right room. Where more than one room is involved, list each one rather than assuming a result from one ceiling describes every surface.

Before booking the plasterer, agree what the asbestos work will leave behind. Removing a coating, taking down a ceiling and reinstating a finished surface are different tasks. Your quote should make clear which are included and which belong to the follow-on trade.

Buying or renovating?

Asbestos floor tile removal: agree the finished scope

Old tiles uncovered beneath carpet or a kitchen floor can interrupt a replacement-flooring job. For asbestos floor tile removal and asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, the key question is what has actually been identified in each layer. Dark adhesive is not automatically a confirmed asbestos material, and a tile result does not establish what is in the adhesive.

Tell us the approximate floor area, whether fitted units or partitions cover part of it, and which rooms need to stay accessible. In a Sutton flat with one entrance, the hallway may also be the only route between the rooms. That affects how the work area and household access need to be discussed before a date is agreed.

Ask your flooring contractor what condition they need the base to be in. Asbestos removal and preparation for a new floor are separate scopes unless expressly included together. Identifying that boundary early helps you compare quotes and avoid paying for a visit that cannot finish the intended stage.

Useful photos

Existing photos showing the room, exposed floor edge and access route are useful. Do not lift another tile or scrape adhesive to get a better picture. Measurements can be approximate for the first enquiry.

Sutton High Street, station-area flats and shared entrances

For premises near Sutton High Street or Sutton Station, give us the entrance used for the actual work. A shop address may have a separate rear service door; a flat above it may have a shared staircase reached from another road. Tell us about that route, the floor level and whoever controls keys or access.

If staff, customers or neighbours still need to use the building, flag that when requesting the quote. The removal area, equipment route and waste route need consideration together. A small amount of material upstairs can involve more planning than the same amount beside a private driveway.

Sutton Council publishes loading and unloading guidance. Check the signs at the proposed stopping point and tell us about loading restrictions. A parking space nearby does not by itself establish a usable route into the affected room.

Garage roofs, internal boards and the right removal route

For asbestos garage roof removal, explain whether the roof alone is coming off or the whole outbuilding is being replaced. Include approximate dimensions, access beside the garage and anything attached beneath the sheets. Replacement roofing is a separate item to confirm, not something to assume from a removal quote.

For asbestos AIB removal around a fireplace, cupboard or ceiling, send the survey description if available. Many asbestos AIB removal and asbestos pipe lagging removal jobs require an HSE-licensed contractor. The material, its condition and the planned method decide the route; you do not need to classify the job yourself.

LOCAL QUESTIONS

Asbestos removal in Sutton town.

Can you quote before I complete a Sutton property purchase?

Yes. Tell us when you expect access and send the relevant survey findings if you have them. We can discuss the removal scope before completion, but any inspection or work needs the property owner’s permission and suitable access. Mention the renovation plans as well as the asbestos finding.

Do I need to have every room tested before calling?

No. Start with what you already know. Explain which rooms will be disturbed and where existing samples were taken. We can then discuss whether further assessment is needed to define the removal work; you do not need to arrange a whole set of tests before making an enquiry.

Can a quote cover both asbestos floor tiles and adhesive?

Yes, where both materials have been identified and the agreed scope includes them. Give us the findings for each layer and describe the floor finish your next contractor needs. The quote should distinguish asbestos floor tile removal, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal and any separate floor preparation.

Can I stay in my Sutton flat during the removal?

That depends on the affected rooms, material, method and whether the work can be separated from the areas you would use. Tell us if the hallway or shared entrance is the only access route. Occupancy arrangements must be agreed for the specific job before work starts.

What if my electrician has already stopped work?

Leave the suspect material undisturbed and call with the location, what has been found and the work the electrician needs to do next. If asbestos is confirmed, we can discuss removal. If it is unconfirmed, assessment comes first. Tell us your programme without assuming an immediate start is possible.

Does the garage roof quote include a replacement roof?

Only if replacement has been specifically agreed. Explain whether another roofer is supplying the new covering or whether the garage is being demolished. Ask for the asbestos removal, waste arrangements and replacement work to be clearly separated so you know who is doing each stage.

What makes one Sutton removal quote different from another?

Compare the actual materials and areas included, access arrangements, protection of occupied spaces, waste handling, any required testing and the handover documents. Check what is excluded too. A lower figure for removing tiles alone is not the same scope as tiles plus confirmed asbestos adhesive.
