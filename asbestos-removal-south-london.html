WHAT IS THE ASBESTOS HOLDING UP?

Plan asbestos removal around the next stage of the South London project.

High-intent enquiries often arrive because something else cannot continue: a flooring contractor is waiting for asbestos floor tile removal, a roofer is waiting for asbestos garage roof removal, or builders cannot continue until asbestos AIB removal or asbestos Artex removal has been dealt with.

Tell us what needs to happen afterwards and whether the building is occupied. In a block of flats or mixed-use property, the handover and shared access can matter as much as the material itself. In a house or garage, access to the work area and the replacement contractor may be the main practical issue.

Survey or no survey?

If asbestos has already been identified, send the relevant report page. If the material is still unconfirmed, tell us what will be disturbed and we can explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Removal South London questions.

Which parts of South London do you cover?

We cover asbestos removal across South London, including Southwark, Lambeth, Wandsworth, Croydon, Sutton, Merton, Lewisham, Greenwich and surrounding areas. Send the full postcode so we can match the enquiry to the right borough or local page.

Do you provide asbestos AIB removal in South London?

Yes. For asbestos AIB removal, send the survey wording if available and tell us where the board is and what work is waiting. Many asbestos AIB removal jobs require an HSE-licensed asbestos contractor, depending on the confirmed material, condition and method.

Do you handle asbestos floor tile removal in South London?

Yes. We take enquiries for asbestos floor tile removal and, where separately confirmed and included, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal. Tell us which rooms or common areas are affected and what flooring is planned next.

Can you help with asbestos Artex removal in South London?

Yes. For asbestos Artex removal and asbestos textured coating removal, tell us whether the coating has been confirmed, which ceilings or walls are affected and what plastering, rewiring or refurbishment is waiting.

Do you remove asbestos garage roofs in South London?

Yes. For asbestos garage roof removal, send safe photos, approximate dimensions and the postcode if available. Tell us whether a roofer, demolition contractor or replacement structure is waiting on the work.

Do I need an asbestos survey before I contact you?

No. If asbestos has already been identified, contact us about removal. If the material has not been confirmed, tell us where it is and what work is planned. We can explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Can you handle commercial asbestos removal in South London?

Yes. We take commercial asbestos removal enquiries for shops, offices, warehouses, managed buildings, plant rooms and refurbishment sites. Tell us about occupancy, access, survey scope and what part of the programme is waiting.

What should I send for a South London asbestos removal quote?

Send the postcode, what asbestos has been identified and what needs to happen next. If available, include survey pages, photos, measurements or drawings. You can still contact us without paperwork.
