FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Pipe Lagging Removal London questions.

Is an asbestos cement flue the same as pipe lagging?

No. Asbestos cement flues are rigid cement products. Pipe lagging and insulation can release fibres more easily, so they need different safety controls and often licensed removal.

Does asbestos pipe insulation require a licensed contractor?

Asbestos pipe lagging is a higher-risk material, and removal normally needs an HSE-licensed contractor. Call us and tell us what needs to come out. We will explain the process.

Can asbestos pipe lagging be left in place or sealed?

Sometimes sound insulation can be managed or enclosed instead of removed. If you need it gone for boiler, heating or building work, call us about asbestos pipe-lagging removal. We will explain the options for the actual job.

Can work be planned in an occupied plant room?

Yes, but the building use, access and nearby equipment all affect the plan. Call us and tell us how the room is used. We will explain how the work can be separated from people and other trades.

Does the heating or boiler system need to be shut down?

Often the affected system must be isolated, cool and safe to work around. The exact shutdown and who carries it out are agreed before asbestos removal, especially in a working plant room.

Can you work with a boiler or heating replacement contractor?

Yes. We can plan the asbestos removal around boiler replacement, heating work or a building-services project. The asbestos area is handed back before engineers continue in that space.

Can I call for an asbestos pipe-lagging removal quote?

Yes. Call us and explain the pipework, rooms and work you are planning. If you already have a survey, register or drawings, you can include them, but you can contact us without paperwork.

Do you remove asbestos pipe lagging across London?

Yes. We remove asbestos pipe lagging from homes, plant rooms and larger buildings across all 32 London boroughs.
