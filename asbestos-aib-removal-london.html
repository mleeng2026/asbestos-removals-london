ASBESTOS AIB REMOVAL FAQ

Questions before you request a quote.

Does all asbestos AIB removal need a licensed contractor?

Most asbestos AIB removal is licensable. Some limited tasks can fall outside licensing, but this depends on the assessed work and the relevant conditions, not just whether the board looks small.

Tell us what the survey says, where the board is and what needs removing. You do not need to decide the category before contacting us. Our licensed asbestos removal information explains the wider service; the actual job determines the controls and planning requirements.

I already have a report identifying asbestos AIB. What do you need for a quote?

Send the relevant report pages, the postcode and a short description of the work you need completed. A survey may identify several items, while your immediate refurbishment affects only part of the property, so tell us which areas the builder needs released.

Approximate quantities, safe photos and access details can help when available. You do not need to assemble a full tender pack. We will discuss any gaps that matter to the asbestos AIB removal quote and whether a site visit is needed.

Can you assess asbestos AIB removal for one panel or a small service area?

Yes. A single panel, doorway section or service cupboard can be discussed as a defined scope. Its condition, fixings, location and the people or equipment nearby all affect assessment; small size does not automatically mean a simple or non-licensed job.

For a doorway or occupied-property enquiry, the asbestos AIB removal page for Tooting explains the access questions. Describe the board in place rather than disturbing it to make the enquiry easier.

Is asbestos insulating board the same as asbestos cement board?

No. Asbestos insulating board, usually shortened to AIB, and asbestos cement are different materials. They can require different controls, so an old board should not be assigned to either category from appearance alone.

If a report identifies the product, send its wording. If it is only suspected, explain the planned work and we can discuss asbestos surveys or sampling to establish the relevant facts. Confirmation can be needed before the removal scope is finalised without being a barrier to first contact.

What affects the cost of asbestos AIB removal in London?

The extent and condition of the board, fixings, working height, access, required controls and surrounding occupancy all affect the quote. Two areas of the same size may involve different arrangements when one is reached directly and the other through managed common parts.

For example, our asbestos AIB removal page for Battersea explains shared access and building-management questions. Tell us which trade follows the asbestos work so exclusions and the required handover are clear before the price is agreed.

What happens before builders return after asbestos AIB removal?

The asbestos work, cleaning and applicable checks must be completed before the affected area is handed back. For work requiring formal clearance, the inspection and testing arrangements form part of planning; not every job has identical documentation.

Agree what will be removed, which areas are excluded and what evidence of completion will be supplied. Builders should not return on the assumption that removing the visible boards is the whole task. Tell us about rewiring, demolition or replacement work that depends on the handover.

Can I discuss asbestos AIB removal without a survey or exact measurements?

Yes. Call 020 8088 0271 or use the enquiry form with the postcode and a short description of the problem. Say whether asbestos AIB has been confirmed or the board is only suspected.

Reports, photographs and approximate measurements are helpful if you already have them, but they are not a checklist you must complete before contacting us. We can discuss the next assessment step and the details needed for a useful quotation.

What should I do if suspected asbestos AIB is uncovered during renovation?

Stop work that could disturb it further and keep people away from the affected area while advice is obtained. Tell us what was uncovered, whether damage or debris is present and which part of the renovation has stopped.

Do not try to remove a piece just to identify it for the enquiry. If other people, tenants or trades use the building, mention that too. We can discuss the assessment and asbestos AIB removal enquiry without assuming the whole refurbishment must be stripped out.
