Different Kingston neighbourhoods need different asbestos-removal planning.

The Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames includes Kingston Town, Surbiton, New Malden, Tolworth, Chessington and other neighbourhoods. A town-centre flat above retail premises, a house in Surbiton, an office in Tolworth and an industrial unit in Chessington can all produce very different access and programme questions even when the asbestos survey identifies the same material.

This borough page is the wider hub. For Kingston Town / KT1, use the dedicated Kingston Town asbestos removal page. Surbiton also has its own page. That hierarchy is deliberate: it lets the borough page cover the full authority without stealing the local purpose of the stronger sub-area pages.

If a survey identifies asbestos AIB removal, send the relevant report wording and tell us which trade is waiting. For asbestos cement flue removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal or asbestos insulation removal, tell us exactly what the survey calls the material so the job is not treated as a generic “pipe” enquiry.

If asbestos has not been confirmed, tell us what part of the building will be disturbed. We can explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step. For the London-wide service, return to Asbestos Removal London.

LOCAL QUESTIONS

Asbestos removal across Kingston upon Thames.

Do you provide asbestos removal across the whole Kingston upon Thames borough?

Yes. We take asbestos removal enquiries across the Royal Borough, including Kingston Town, Surbiton, New Malden, Tolworth, Chessington and surrounding areas. Send the postcode and nearest local area so the enquiry can be matched to the right location and access context.

Is the Kingston Town page different from this borough page?

Yes. This page is the borough-wide hub. The Kingston Town / KT1 page is deliberately narrower and focuses on Kingston Town, Wood Street, Clarence Street, Eden Street and the immediate town-centre area. We keep both because they serve different local search intent.

Do you handle asbestos AIB removal in Kingston upon Thames?

Yes. For asbestos AIB removal, send the survey wording if available and tell us where the board is, how much is affected and what work is waiting. Many asbestos AIB removal jobs require an HSE-licensed asbestos contractor, so the confirmed material, condition and method matter.

Can you help with asbestos cement flue removal or asbestos pipe lagging removal?

Yes. We take enquiries for asbestos cement flue removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal and asbestos insulation removal, but these are not the same material or risk category. Send the report wording, photos or drawings if available and tell us what heating, boiler or refurbishment work is waiting.

What other asbestos removal services do you cover in the borough?

Enquiries can include asbestos Artex removal, asbestos textured coating removal, asbestos floor tile removal, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, asbestos garage roof removal, asbestos roof removal and commercial asbestos removal work.

Do I need an asbestos survey before refurbishment in Kingston upon Thames?

If asbestos has already been identified, contact us about removal. If the material is unknown and refurbishment will disturb the building, tell us what work is planned and we can explain whether an asbestos refurbishment survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Can you work with landlords, managing agents and commercial contractors?

Yes. We take enquiries from landlords, property managers, facilities teams, main contractors, developers and business owners. Tell us who occupies the building, which areas are affected and what part of the programme or handover is waiting on the asbestos work.

What should I send for a Kingston upon Thames asbestos removal quote?

Send the property postcode, nearest local area, what asbestos has been identified and what needs to happen next. If available, include survey pages, sample results, photos, measurements or drawings. You can still contact us if you do not have paperwork yet.
