ALREADY REMOVED OR STILL IN PLACE?

The answer changes the asbestos disposal plan.

If asbestos material is still fixed to the property, tell us what needs removing and what is happening afterwards. A garage roof that must come off before an outbuilding is replaced is a different job from packaged waste already produced by controlled removal.

If the waste is already on site, tell us what the material is believed to be, how it was removed, how it is contained, the approximate quantity and whether you have any survey, sample or consignment paperwork. We can then assess whether the enquiry is suitable for collection or whether another step is needed first.

For material that has not been confirmed, the useful next step may be an asbestos survey, sample or test before removal or disposal is planned. You can still contact us before you have that paperwork.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Disposal London questions.

Can you collect asbestos waste in London?

We take asbestos waste collection and disposal enquiries across London. Tell us what the material is, whether it has already been removed, how it is contained, roughly how much there is and the postcode. We can then assess the correct next step.

What if the asbestos is still fixed to the building?

If the asbestos is still attached to a roof, flue, tank, wall, ceiling or another part of the property, tell us what needs removing. The job may need to be planned as asbestos removal first, with lawful transport and disposal following as part of the work.

Do I need an asbestos survey before I ask about disposal?

No. If the material has already been identified, send the relevant asbestos survey or sample result if you have it. If the material has not been confirmed, you can still contact us and explain where it is and what work is planned. We can explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Can you dispose of asbestos garage roof sheets?

Yes, we take enquiries involving asbestos garage roof removal and the resulting asbestos cement sheet waste. If the roof is still in place, send photos, approximate dimensions and the postcode so the removal and disposal can be assessed together.

What paperwork should come with asbestos disposal?

Lawful asbestos waste transport and disposal should be supported by the relevant waste documentation for the work, including hazardous-waste consignment documentation where applicable. If paperwork is important for a landlord, contractor, facilities team or project file, tell us at the start.

Can you help with commercial asbestos waste?

Yes. We take commercial asbestos removal and disposal enquiries from offices, shops, warehouses, managed buildings, plant areas and refurbishment sites across London. Tell us the survey scope, access, occupancy and what programme or trade is waiting.

What should I send for an asbestos disposal quote?

Send the London postcode, what material is involved, whether it is still fixed in place or already removed, the approximate amount and any photos or survey/sample information you already have. If another trade or project stage is waiting, tell us that too.

Do you cover asbestos disposal across Greater London?

Yes. We take asbestos disposal and asbestos removal enquiries across all 32 London boroughs and Greater London. If you are unsure whether your location or waste type is covered, send the postcode and a short description of the job.
