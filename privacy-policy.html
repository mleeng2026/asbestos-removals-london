PRIVACY & UK GDPR

Privacy Policy

Asbestos Removals London UK uses the personal information you send through this website to respond to your enquiry, discuss the work and prepare or arrange a quote.

Information we collect

This may include your name, telephone number, email address, property location, job details and any photographs, surveys or documents you choose to upload.

How we use and share it

We use your information to deal with your enquiry and take steps you request before arranging asbestos services. We may share the relevant details with the contractor or service provider handling the enquiry and with suppliers that operate this website and its forms, including Formspree. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and business records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide the service, resolve questions and meet legal or accounting duties. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or securely disposed of.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where those rights apply. To make a request, call 020 8088 0271 or use the contact form. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use essential technology and Google advertising or measurement tools. Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies, those tools should not be activated until that consent has been given.
