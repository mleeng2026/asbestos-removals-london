FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Water Tank Removal London questions.

Do you remove asbestos water tanks across London?

Yes. We remove asbestos cement water tanks from lofts, cupboards, plant areas and other parts of buildings across all 32 London boroughs.

What if the asbestos water tank is bigger than the loft hatch?

That is common with old asbestos water tanks in lofts. We plan how the asbestos water tank can be taken out safely when it will not pass through the hatch. Do not try to break it up yourself. Call us and tell us about the access.

Does the asbestos water tank need disconnecting first?

Usually the asbestos water tank must be drained and disconnected before asbestos removal. A plumber may need to do that part. Call us and we will explain the order of work.

Can an asbestos water tank be removed whole?

Sometimes. It depends on the asbestos water tank size, route through the loft and hatch opening. If it cannot come out whole, the asbestos removal method must control breakage and asbestos fibres.

Do I need another test if the water tank is already confirmed as asbestos?

No, not automatically. If the water tank is already confirmed as asbestos cement, call us about asbestos water tank removal. If you have a survey or sample result, you can include it, but you do not need to arrange another test first.

Can a redundant asbestos tank stay in the loft?

A sound asbestos water tank that will not be disturbed can sometimes be left in place, but it should remain recorded and undamaged. If you want the asbestos water tank removed, call us and we will talk through the job.

Can I call for an asbestos water tank removal quote?

Yes. Call us and explain where the asbestos water tank is, whether it is connected and how the loft or room is reached. We will ask for anything else needed to quote.

What affects the price of asbestos water tank removal?

The asbestos water tank size, location, access, disconnection and whether it can come out whole all affect the price. Call us and talk through the job with a real person.
