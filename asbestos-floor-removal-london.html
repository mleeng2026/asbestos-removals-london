FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Floor Tile Removal London questions.

Do old floor tiles contain asbestos?

Some older vinyl tiles, backing materials and bitumen adhesives contain asbestos, but not every old floor does. If asbestos is already confirmed, call us about removal. If the material is unknown and it will be disturbed, a sample may be needed.

Can you remove asbestos floor tiles before new flooring?

Yes. We remove asbestos floor tiles before new flooring is fitted. Call us and tell us which rooms need work and what you plan to put down afterwards.

Do you remove the black adhesive under asbestos tiles?

Yes, when the black adhesive has been confirmed as asbestos-containing. It can be removed with the tiles or dealt with as a separate part of the job. We will explain what is practical for your floor.

Can I call for an asbestos floor removal quote?

Yes. Call us and explain which rooms or asbestos floor areas need work. If you already have a survey or sample result, you can include it.

Can new flooring go over old asbestos floor tiles?

Sometimes sound tiles can be left in place and covered, but that is not right for every floor or refurbishment. If you want the old asbestos floor tiles removed first, call us and we will talk through the job.

Does asbestos floor tile removal require a licensed contractor?

Careful removal of intact asbestos floor tiles can often be non-licensed work by trained workers. Paper backing, badly broken tiles or work on the adhesive may need different controls. Call us and we will explain what applies.

How will the floor be left after asbestos floor tile removal?

We agree the finish before work starts. The asbestos floor tiles and any agreed backing or adhesive are removed, and we explain what preparation may still be needed before the new floor is fitted.

Can you remove asbestos floor tiles from several rooms or a whole building?

Yes. We remove asbestos floor tiles from one room, several rooms or larger floor areas across all 32 London boroughs. Call us and tell us about the property.
