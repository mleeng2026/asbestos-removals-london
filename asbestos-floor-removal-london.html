Which floor layers need to be included?

A report may identify asbestos in a tile without testing the backing or adhesive. Tell us exactly which layer the result covers. If several rooms have different tiles, later repairs or different residues, describe those differences rather than treating the whole property as one confirmed material.

For asbestos bitumen adhesive removal after tiles have already been lifted, the useful starting point is the remaining floor area, its condition and any result for the adhesive itself. Dark residue in a photograph does not establish asbestos. You can discuss asbestos sampling before flooring work without lifting or scraping anything for the enquiry.

How will people get through the property?

A hallway or shop entrance can be a through-route as well as part of the floor being replaced. Tell us which rooms, stairs or trading areas depend on it, and whether anyone needs to remain in the building. Dividing the work into rooms is only useful if the access arrangements and assessment support it.

In a London block, include the floor level, service entrance and any lift-booking arrangements you already know. For premises on a red route, a loading bay should not be assumed to provide parking for the whole job: TfL explains the loading times and restrictions. We can discuss the actual route for equipment and removed materials when assessing the work.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Floor Tile Removal London questions.

Do old floor tiles and black adhesive always contain asbestos?

No. Neither the age or appearance of a tile nor the black colour of an adhesive confirms asbestos. Tiles, backing and adhesive are separate layers, and evidence for one layer should not automatically be applied to another.

If the materials are unidentified and will be disturbed, we can discuss asbestos sampling before flooring work. Leave them in place for the enquiry; safe photos can explain the layout without lifting tiles or scraping residue to provide evidence.

Can you arrange asbestos floor tile removal before new flooring is fitted?

Yes. Tell us which rooms need work and what the flooring installer plans to fit. The asbestos floor tile removal quote should identify the included area and whether backing or asbestos bitumen adhesive removal is also required.

Agree what floor condition the installer needs to receive. Completion of the asbestos scope does not automatically include levelling, screeding or every preparation step for the new flooring system. A clear division of responsibility helps avoid a gap between contractors.

Can you quote for asbestos bitumen adhesive removal after the tiles are gone?

Yes. Adhesive residue can be assessed as a separate scope when the tiles have already been removed. Tell us what evidence exists for asbestos in the adhesive itself and what flooring is planned next.

Black residue alone is not confirmation. The asbestos floor tile removal and asbestos bitumen adhesive removal page for Uxbridge explains the separate-layer issue and handover. Avoid arranging grinding or sanding of unidentified adhesive before it has been assessed.

Can I get an asbestos floor tile removal quote without exact measurements?

Yes. Start with the postcode, the rooms involved and rough dimensions if known. Say whether the floor is intact, partly lifted or damaged, and whether anyone is using the rooms.

An existing survey or sample result can help, but you do not need paperwork before first contact. Explain any deadline or waiting flooring contractor so the enquiry addresses both the material and the wider project. We can ask for the additional detail needed before the scope is agreed.

Can new flooring go over existing asbestos floor tiles?

In some circumstances sound materials can remain in place and be managed, but suitability depends on their condition and the proposed flooring work. Leaving tiles in place does not make later drilling, sanding or disturbance risk-free.

If your project needs asbestos floor tile removal, explain the reason and the intended floor finish. We can discuss that scope rather than assuming covering is suitable. The decision should account for both asbestos considerations and the requirements of the next flooring contractor.

Does asbestos floor tile removal always require licensed work?

No. Some asbestos floor tile removal is non-licensed work, but the material, condition and intended method must be assessed. Different backing materials, damage or work on other layers can change the requirements.

Do not choose a category from the tile’s appearance or assume a previous job sets the rule for yours. We can discuss the confirmed findings and proposed task. Our licensed asbestos removal information covers the wider distinction without treating every asbestos material as the same job.

How will the floor be left after asbestos floor tile removal?

The handover should reflect the written scope: which tiles, backing and adhesive are included, what cleaning and checks apply, and what remains for the next contractor. Asbestos bitumen adhesive removal should be named separately where it forms part of the work.

Ask about any remaining preparation before agreeing a new flooring date. A completed asbestos scope is not a guarantee that the substrate meets every levelling, moisture or finish requirement of the chosen flooring system.

Can asbestos floor tile removal cover several rooms or commercial premises?

Yes. Enquiries can cover one room, several rooms and larger premises across London. Tell us whether all areas are available together or whether occupants and business activity mean access must be considered in stages.

For shops, offices or managed premises, the route through the building and the next flooring programme matter alongside total area. See commercial asbestos removal in London for wider commercial scopes, including projects where more than one asbestos material is involved.
