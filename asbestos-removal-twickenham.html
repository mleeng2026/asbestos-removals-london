Plan the next stage of your Twickenham project

Garage roof replacement: decide what stays before asking for a price

A Twickenham garage enquiry should begin with the intended finished structure. If the frame and walls are staying, say so. If the entire garage is being removed before a new building or garden project, identify that wider scope. Roof sheets, wall panels, gutters and contents should not be silently combined into one roof measurement.

Send approximate dimensions already available and ground-level photographs of the structure and approach. Mention shared drives, side passages, gates and adjoining buildings. Do not climb onto suspect roof sheets for measurements. The contractor needs to understand the route as well as the material.

Coordinate the handover with the replacement roofer or builder. Repairs to retained framing, temporary weather protection and a new covering may belong to separate contractors. Ask the proposal to state those boundaries so the removal visit does not leave the next stage waiting on work nobody included.

London Road and King Street buildings with shared access

For a flat, shop or office near London Road, King Street or Twickenham station, give the actual entrance to the work. A shop front, rear service door and upstairs residential doorway can serve different occupiers at the same postal address. Identify who can authorise the route needed.

Tell us whether residents, staff or customers remain in adjoining areas. Access and occupancy must be considered alongside the proposed method. A small internal item does not automatically mean shared corridors or the rest of the premises can stay in normal use throughout the work.

If a managing agent has requirements for appointments, keys, lifts or service isolation, include them at quotation stage. For a fit-out, show the survey findings against the proposed changes. An inaccessible area that the builder intends to open needs attention before the strip-out progresses into it.

Floors, boards and the next stage inside the property

For asbestos floor tile removal, identify the rooms and any separately confirmed adhesive. The flooring installer should explain the base they need afterwards. Asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, surface repair and laying the new floor are different activities unless explicitly packaged together.

Asbestos AIB removal around a fireplace, cupboard or ceiling needs the report description and intended alteration. Many AIB and pipe lagging tasks require licensed work, with the material, condition and method determining the appropriate route. Tell us about services crossing the area and who will arrange any necessary isolation.

Asbestos removal in Twickenham: practical questions

Can a Twickenham quote cover a roof without dismantling the garage?

Yes, a roof-only scope can be assessed. Explain which frame and wall elements remain and what the replacement roofer requires. The proposal should identify the retained structure and any separate repairs or protection, rather than leave the boundary implied by the words “garage removal”.

Where should I enquire specifically about textured ceilings?

Use our dedicated asbestos Artex removal Twickenham page for ceiling and wall coating enquiries, or call the same team. Identify each surface, available sample results and planned disturbance. If the property has other asbestos items as well, include them separately in the wider enquiry.

Can work be planned in a shop with a flat above it?

Yes, the arrangement can be assessed. Tell us which entrances, services and routes are shared and which spaces remain occupied. Include the landlord or manager where they control access. Continued trading or occupation must be considered for the proposed method, not assumed from the floor on which removal occurs.

What if a roof contractor is already booked?

Tell us the date and what they expect to receive, but keep their booking provisional until the removal scope and arrangements are agreed. Establish who handles access equipment, repairs and temporary protection where needed. Both contractors should understand the same handover boundary.

Can I get help with an unknown material before refurbishment?

Yes. Describe its location and the alteration planned, and share existing photographs without disturbing it further. A picture cannot confirm composition. We can discuss whether survey or sampling information is needed before an appropriate removal method and reliable proposal can be established.

Do you cover both TW1 and TW2 addresses?

Yes. We take Twickenham enquiries across both postcode areas. Provide the full address and actual work entrance, particularly for a flat or subdivided premises. Our Richmond upon Thames borough page also covers other local areas if the project extends beyond Twickenham.
