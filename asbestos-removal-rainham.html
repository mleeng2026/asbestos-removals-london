Industrial roofs around the Ferry Lane area

A commercial asbestos roof removal enquiry should identify the unit, building use and roof construction. Asbestos removal in Rainham: practical questions

Is this Rainham in Havering or Rainham in Kent?

This page covers Rainham in the London Borough of Havering, including RM13. Send the full postcode and site address so the enquiry is assigned correctly. For a commercial estate, add the unit number and actual entrance rather than relying on the estate name alone.

Can you quote for only one section of an industrial roof?

Yes, a section-specific enquiry can be assessed. Mark the boundary on a plan and provide the relevant survey entries. Explain how it joins the retained roof and how replacement work will follow. The quotation should state the agreed extent and any assumptions about adjoining construction.

Can vehicles keep using the yard during removal?

That depends on the work area, equipment, access and method. Show the normal vehicle routes and identify operations you want to retain. Suitable arrangements must be assessed for the site; a large yard does not by itself mean vehicle movements can continue close to the work.

Does roof removal include sheets already stacked on site?

Only if those items are disclosed and included in the proposal. Give their location, condition and any identification information. Installed sheets and detached waste are different parts of the enquiry, so do not assume a roof measurement covers everything stored elsewhere.

What does the replacement roofer need to know?

They need the removal boundary, expected condition of the retained structure and agreed release point. Identify who supplies access equipment, repairs and temporary weather protection where required. These responsibilities should be coordinated before either contractor confirms a programme based on the other’s assumed work.

Do you also handle a small domestic ceiling job in RM13?

Yes. Tell us the room, sample result if available and the electrical or plastering work planned. We take domestic asbestos Artex removal enquiries as well as commercial work. The next step depends on the actual finding and intended disturbance rather than the size of the business enquiry list.
