When asbestos AIB is holding up work in a Tooting property

Asbestos AIB removal becomes a live buying need when a ceiling panel, partition, service-boxing area or other board has been identified and a kitchen, electrical, heating or general refurbishment project cannot safely continue around it. The important information is what the report actually says and which part of the project is blocked.

You do not need to translate a survey into technical language for us. Send the relevant wording if you have it. If you do not, tell us what was found, where it is and what you need to do next. That keeps the enquiry practical and avoids making paperwork a barrier.

Occupied homes, shared entrances and Tooting refurbishment work

For an upper-floor property near Tooting Broadway, Tooting High Street or Mitcham Road, tell us whether the entrance is shared with a shop, business or another flat. Confirmed asbestos AIB or an unidentified board?

Questions about asbestos AIB removal in Tooting

Can you assess one panel above a doorway in Tooting?

Yes. Tell us where the panel is, how it is fixed if known and whether people need to pass through that doorway. If a report confirms asbestos AIB, send that finding. A small panel still needs assessment; its size alone does not settle the controls or licensing requirement.

Can I ask for a quote if I only suspect the board contains asbestos?

Yes. Tell us where the material is and what work is planned. If confirmation is needed before an asbestos AIB removal quote can be finalised, we will explain that next step.

Can asbestos AIB removal be planned while a Tooting property is occupied?

It depends on the location and scope. Tell us which rooms are occupied, how the work area is reached and whether other people or trades need nearby access so the requirements can be assessed properly.

What if the electrician needs the service area before the rest of the refurbishment?

Tell us which area the electrician needs and what work is waiting. We can discuss whether that area can form a clearly defined asbestos AIB removal scope, what access is needed and what checks apply before it is handed back. An enquiry does not commit you to stripping the whole property.

Do I need to decide whether the job is licensed before I call?

No. The licensing requirement is determined from the confirmed material and work scope. You do not need to choose a category before contacting us.
