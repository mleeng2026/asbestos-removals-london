BUYER QUESTIONS

Questions about asbestos AIB removal in Tooting

Can you assess a single panel above a doorway for asbestos AIB removal in Tooting?

Yes. A single panel can be assessed as a defined job; you do not need to be planning a whole-property strip-out. Its condition, fixings and position above a frequently used doorway affect how the work can be planned.

Tell us whether that doorway is the only route to other rooms or an upstairs flat. Leave the panel in place for assessment rather than removing it to provide a photograph. Our asbestos AIB removal service across London covers both small defined scopes and larger areas.

What if the suspected board is beside electrical equipment or inside a service cupboard?

Tell us about the equipment as well as the board. Access to electrical services, any necessary isolation and the asbestos work need to be coordinated; a board photograph alone does not settle those arrangements or confirm its material.

If an electrician has already stopped work, explain what they need to reach and whether the installation is still in use. We can discuss the asbestos AIB removal enquiry alongside that requirement. Electrical disconnection or reinstatement should not be assumed to be included in the asbestos quotation.

Can asbestos AIB removal be arranged in a flat above a Tooting shop?

It needs assessment around both the affected area and the people using the building. For a flat above premises on Tooting High Street or Mitcham Road, tell us whether the residential entrance is separate, whether stairs are shared and whether the shop remains open.

These details can change access planning and the people who need to agree it. We do not assume that closing one room makes the whole arrangement suitable. For other materials in the same property, see asbestos removal in Tooting.

Can the electrician’s area be dealt with before the rest of the refurbishment?

A defined area can be discussed separately, provided the assessment supports that scope and its boundaries are clear. Explain which board or service area is preventing the electrician from continuing, and what other work is planned later.

The quote should identify what is included, what remains untouched and the checks needed before that area is released. Asbestos AIB removal does not automatically include later electrical work, replacement boards or decoration. Agreeing those boundaries is more useful than assuming every room must be stripped at once.

Do I need a survey or a licensing decision before asking for an asbestos AIB removal quote?

No. Start with the postcode, the position of the board and the work you want to complete. If you have a survey or sample result, send the relevant part; if you only suspect asbestos, say so.

We can discuss the evidence still needed and the appropriate assessment route. Most asbestos AIB removal is licensable, but the decision belongs with the assessment of the actual task, not a guess from its size. See asbestos surveys and sampling in London if the material is still unidentified.

Can people use the doorway or nearby rooms while asbestos AIB removal takes place?

Do not assume they can. The work area, access arrangements and controls must be decided for the particular job. A panel over a doorway may affect a route used by everyone in the property, even if the amount of board is small.

Tell us which rooms people need, whether there is another entrance and any tenant or building-management involvement. We can explain what needs agreeing before a date is set. You can discuss your Tooting property and planned work without having solved those arrangements first.
