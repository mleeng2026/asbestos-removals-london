FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about asbestos soffit removal.

Are asbestos soffits usually AIB or asbestos cement?

Both materials were used. They are different asbestos products and the removal controls are not the same, so tell us what has already been confirmed.

What information do you need to quote for asbestos soffit removal?

Tell us the building type, height, approximate length of soffit, material if known, access below and what fascia, gutter or roofline work is planned afterwards.

Does asbestos AIB soffit removal require a licensed contractor?

AIB is a higher-risk asbestos material and many AIB removal jobs are licensable. The exact classification depends on the material, condition and work. We will explain what applies.

Can you remove soffits from blocks of flats or managed buildings?

Yes. For larger buildings, we consider the height, residents, access equipment, parking, public areas and when the roofline work is due to start.

What if a fascia contractor finds asbestos soffits after starting work?

Stop work that could disturb the board. Tell us what has been found, where it is and what roofline work the contractor is planning. Existing photos, reports or sample results are useful if available.

Will scaffolding or a platform be needed?

It depends on the height, ground below, soffit position, length of the run and obstructions such as extensions or conservatories. We will discuss access as part of the quote.

Can the new fascia and soffit system be fitted after asbestos removal?

Yes. We remove the asbestos soffits first. The roofline contractor can begin when the asbestos work is complete.

Do you remove asbestos soffits across London?

Yes. We take asbestos soffit removal enquiries across all 32 London boroughs for homes, flats, estates and commercial or managed buildings.
