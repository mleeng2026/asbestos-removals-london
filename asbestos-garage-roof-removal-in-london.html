ROOF CONDITION AND ACCESS

Show us the roof and the route to it.

A photo of the whole roof and a close view taken from a safe position help us understand the sheets, edges and access. Do not climb onto an ageing cement roof or disturb broken pieces to take a photo. Tell us if the sheets have a hole, loose edges or damage after a fire; the condition affects how the job is planned.

On a terraced property, access may be through the house, a rear alley or a neighbour's route. Mention parking restrictions and anything close to the roof, such as a fence, another garage or the house wall. If the roof joins a neighbouring garage, we will need to understand where your sheets end and the adjoining covering begins.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about asbestos garage roof removal.

Do you remove asbestos garage roofs across London?

Yes. We remove asbestos cement roofs from single, double, attached and shared garages across all 32 London boroughs.

Can you remove only the asbestos roof and leave the garage standing?

Yes. Roof-only removal is a common job. We remove the agreed asbestos cement sheets and leave the suitable garage structure in place for the replacement roof.

What information do you need to quote?

Tell us whether the garage is single, double, attached or shared, the approximate roof size, sheet condition, access, postcode and whether another roofer is waiting. Photos are useful if you have them.

Can you remove a leaking, cracked or fire-damaged asbestos garage roof?

Yes. Damaged sheets need more care than intact sheets. Tell us what has happened and do not walk on or break the roof.

Can my replacement roofer start straight after the asbestos roof is removed?

We normally remove the asbestos sheets first, then your roofer fits the new roof. Tell us if the roofer is already booked.

What if the garage roof is attached to my house or joined to a neighbour's garage?

Tell us when you call. We need to understand the join, access and how the adjoining area will be left protected when the asbestos sheets are removed.

Will I receive asbestos waste paperwork?

Yes. The agreed job includes lawful transport and disposal of the asbestos cement waste, with the relevant waste paperwork supplied.

What affects the price of asbestos garage-roof removal?

Roof size, sheet condition, height, access, whether the garage is attached or shared, and the removal scope all affect the price.
