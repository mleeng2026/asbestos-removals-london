LICENSED & NON-LICENSED WORK

Need licensed asbestos removal in London?

Not every asbestos removal job needs an HSE-licensed contractor. The material, condition and way the work will disturb it decide the correct route. Higher-risk work commonly includes asbestos pipe lagging removal, asbestos insulation removal and many asbestos AIB removal jobs.

Our licensed asbestos removal work is carried out under Best Environmental Solutions Ltd's current standard HSE asbestos licence, number 152405768, valid until 10 March 2027. If you already have a survey, send the relevant page. If you do not, tell us what has been found and what you need to do next.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about
asbestos removal in London.

What asbestos removal work can you help with?

We take asbestos removal enquiries for asbestos AIB removal, asbestos Artex removal, asbestos textured coating removal, asbestos floor tile removal, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, asbestos garage roof removal, asbestos roof removal, asbestos AIB soffit removal, asbestos cement flue removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal and other confirmed asbestos materials in homes and commercial buildings across London.

Do I need an asbestos survey before I contact you?

No. If asbestos has already been identified, call us about the removal. If the material has not been confirmed, tell us where it is and what work you are planning. We will explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Do you provide licensed asbestos removal in London?

Yes. Our licensed asbestos removal work is carried out under Best Environmental Solutions Ltd's current standard HSE asbestos licence, number 152405768, valid until 10 March 2027. Not every job needs licensed work; the material, condition and removal method determine the correct route.

Can you quote from an asbestos survey?

Yes. Send the relevant survey or asbestos-register pages, the London postcode and tell us what needs to be removed. Photos, measurements and drawings can also help where you already have them.

What if asbestos is holding up builders or refurbishment work?

Tell us which trade or project stage is waiting and what asbestos has been found. We regularly receive enquiries where roofing, rewiring, strip-out, ceiling replacement, demolition or refurbishment cannot continue until the asbestos work is dealt with.

Do you handle commercial asbestos removal?

Yes. We take commercial asbestos removal enquiries for offices, shops, warehouses, plant rooms, managed buildings, refurbishment sites and larger asbestos cement roofs across London.

Do you cover asbestos removal near me across London?

We cover asbestos removal enquiries across all 32 London boroughs and Greater London. Send the postcode with your enquiry so the job can be assessed in the right local context.

What affects an asbestos removal quote?

The material, amount, condition, access, height, property type, occupancy, controls required and what needs to happen afterwards can all affect the quote. Give us what you know and we will ask for anything else that matters.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Send the London postcode and a short description of what needs removing. If you have photos, measurements, an asbestos survey, sample result, asbestos register or drawings, include the useful parts. You can still contact us without paperwork.
