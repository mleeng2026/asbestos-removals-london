Questions about
asbestos removal in London.

What asbestos removal work can you help with?

We provide asbestos removal across London for a wide range of materials and property types. Typical enquiries include asbestos AIB removal, asbestos Artex removal and asbestos textured coating removal, asbestos floor tile removal and asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, asbestos garage roof removal, asbestos roof removal, asbestos AIB soffit removal, asbestos cement flue removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal, asbestos insulation removal and asbestos cement sheet removal in homes and commercial buildings.

Recent enquiries have ranged from asbestos AIB affecting ceilings before a family moved into a property, to asbestos floor tiles and asbestos textured coatings being dealt with before refurbishment, and asbestos materials that needed removing before builders or other trades could continue.

Do I need an asbestos survey before I contact you?

No. If asbestos has already been identified, contact us about the asbestos removal work.

If the material has not been confirmed, tell us where it is and what work you are planning. We can explain whether an asbestos survey, asbestos sample or asbestos test is the sensible next step.

For example, we regularly receive enquiries where a homeowner knows old floor tiles or textured coatings are present but wants confirmation before removal and replacement work begins.

Do you provide licensed asbestos removal in London?

Yes. We provide licensed asbestos removal in London for work that requires an HSE-licensed asbestos contractor.

Not every asbestos removal job requires licensed work. The asbestos material, its condition, the type of work and how it needs to be removed determine the correct route.

Recent higher-risk enquiries have included asbestos AIB removal where the asbestos needed to be removed before other trades could begin work.

If you need the appointed contractor’s current HSE licence details for your records, tender or project file, call 020 8088 0271 or email info@asbestoslondon.co.uk and we can discuss the details needed for your project.

Can you quote from an asbestos survey?

Yes. Send us the relevant asbestos survey or asbestos-register pages, the property postcode and details of what needs to be removed.

Photos, measurements and drawings can also help us assess the job where you already have them.

We regularly receive enquiries where the survey already identifies the asbestos material and approximate area, such as asbestos AIB removal across a ceiling, asbestos floor tile removal, or asbestos materials that must be dealt with before refurbishment starts.

What if asbestos is holding up builders or refurbishment work?

Tell us what asbestos has been found and which trade or stage of the project is waiting.

We regularly deal with asbestos removal enquiries where roofing, rewiring, soft strip, ceiling replacement, demolition or refurbishment cannot continue until the asbestos work has been dealt with.

One recent enquiry involved asbestos AIB removal that was a prerequisite before other trades could start. Other enquiries have involved asbestos ceilings, asbestos floor tiles and asbestos textured coatings that needed dealing with before renovation or replacement work could continue.

If you already have a survey, photos or measurements, send them with the enquiry.

Do you handle commercial asbestos removal?

Yes. We provide commercial asbestos removal in London for offices, shops, warehouses, plant rooms, managed buildings, refurbishment sites and other commercial properties.

What affects an asbestos removal quote?

The asbestos material, amount, condition, access, height, property type, whether the building is occupied, the controls required and what needs to happen afterwards can all affect an asbestos removal quote.

For example, asbestos AIB removal from a ceiling inside a house is a very different job from asbestos garage roof removal or removing asbestos materials from a larger commercial building.

Send us what you know about the job and we will ask for anything else needed to assess it properly.
