Garage roofs in Bexleyheath

If a roofer is ready to replace a garage roof, tell us whether the roof alone is coming off or the whole garage is being taken down. Let us know what the survey identified, roughly how big the roof is and whether the roof joins another building. A couple of photographs taken from the ground will show the access and the shape of the building. There is no need to climb onto the roof or disturb it to get a better picture.

Some garages have corrugated roof sheets as well as wall cladding or a lining inside. Tell us which of those parts are included in the building work. If the garage frame is staying, the roofer needs to know what will be left ready for the replacement roof. If the entire building is coming down, that is a different job. We can talk through asbestos garage roof removal once we know exactly what is being removed.

Floor tiles in a Welling renovation

Old floor tiles often come up in the middle of a purchase or refurbishment. A survey may identify the tiles but leave the adhesive underneath untested. If you have a report, send the relevant pages and tell us which rooms are being renovated. A black adhesive cannot be identified as asbestos just by looking at it. The flooring contractor may be lifting a kitchen floor, a hallway or several rooms, so a simple marked plan is more useful than one overall measurement.

Tell us what is due to happen after the floor work: new tiles, another covering or a larger strip-out. Mention any kitchen units, counters or skirting that are staying in place. If you have not completed the purchase, say when the seller can provide access. We can discuss asbestos floor tile removal for confirmed material and whether asbestos bitumen adhesive removal is part of the job. If a layer has not been checked, we will say what needs confirming before it can be included in a removal quote.

Asbestos found at an Erith business property

A survey for a unit in Erith may list roof sheets, boards and pipe insulation in different parts of the building. You do not need to request removal of everything in the report. Mark the items that the current work will disturb and send those pages to us. If a roofer is waiting, identify the roof section. If the work is indoors, give the room or service area and say whether staff or other contractors will still be on site.

Access matters too. Tell us who can open the area, whether equipment or stock is in the way, and which job must be finished before the next trade starts. Asbestos AIB removal and asbestos roof removal are different jobs; a general photo of the building is not enough to price either one. We can start with the confirmed findings, discuss whether a visit would help and keep the quote focused on the work you actually need.

Questions about asbestos removal in Bexley

Can I call if I have not had an asbestos survey?

Yes. Tell us what you have found and what work is planned. If the material has not been confirmed, we can explain whether a survey or sample is the next step. Leave it undisturbed in the meantime.

Can you quote for just a garage roof in Bexleyheath?

Yes. Tell us if the frame and walls are staying, give rough roof dimensions and send the survey finding if available. A full garage demolition would be quoted as a separate scope.

Does a Welling floor survey cover the adhesive too?

Only if the report says so. Tiles and the adhesive beneath them can be separate findings. Send the relevant pages and tell us which rooms the flooring contractor will lift.

Do you handle commercial asbestos removal around Erith?

Yes. Send the survey item numbers for the roof or rooms affected by the planned works. Let us know whether the premises will remain in use and who can arrange access.
