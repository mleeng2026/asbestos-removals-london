When a Bexleyheath garage roof is holding up the roofer

If the roofer is ready to start but the old garage roof has been identified as asbestos cement, send us the survey page or sample result and a couple of photographs taken safely from ground level. We need to know roughly how big the roof is, how we can get to it and whether the garage is attached to the house or another building.

It also matters whether you are replacing just the roof or clearing the whole garage. Those are different jobs. If the frame and walls are staying, tell us that from the start so the removal quote covers what the roofer actually needs.

If sheets are already cracked or there are loose pieces on the ground, mention that as well. There is no reason to climb onto the roof or move material around for better photographs. Once we know the scope, we can tell you what else we need to quote for asbestos garage roof removal.

Floor tiles found during a Welling renovation

Flooring jobs often stop halfway through planning because a survey has picked up old tiles. If that has happened in a house or flat around Welling, send the relevant part of the report and tell us which rooms are being stripped.

Do not assume the black adhesive underneath is covered by the same result. The tiles and adhesive can be separate findings. If both have been tested, send both results. If only the tiles were sampled, tell us that and we can keep the quote clear about what is confirmed and what still needs checking.

A rough room-by-room area is more useful than one total figure, especially if kitchen units, skirting or other fittings are staying. If another flooring contractor is booked in, give us the date they are hoping to start. We can then scope asbestos floor tile removal around the work that actually needs to happen.

A survey has flagged asbestos at an Erith commercial property

A commercial survey can contain several asbestos items even when the planned works only affect one part of the building. If you are dealing with a unit or business premises around Erith, send us the item numbers that relate to the work rather than assuming everything in the report has to come out.

For example, roof sheets, AIB around services and pipe insulation are not one generic “asbestos job”. Tell us which area contractors need back, whether the premises will stay in use and who controls access. If there is a drawing or marked-up survey plan, include it.

That lets us talk about the actual removal scope and programme instead of quoting from a postcode and a photograph of the outside of the building. For larger premises, see our commercial asbestos removal information.

Questions about asbestos removal in Bexley

Can I call if I have not had an asbestos survey?

Yes. Tell us what you have found and what work is planned. If the material has not been confirmed, we can explain whether a survey or sample is the next step. Leave it undisturbed in the meantime.

Can you quote for just a garage roof in Bexleyheath?

Yes. Tell us if the frame and walls are staying, give rough roof dimensions and send the survey finding if available. A full garage demolition would be quoted as a separate scope.

Does a Welling floor survey cover the adhesive too?

Only if the report says so. Tiles and the adhesive beneath them can be separate findings. Send the relevant pages and tell us which rooms the flooring contractor will lift.

Do you handle commercial asbestos removal around Erith?

Yes. Send the survey item numbers for the roof or rooms affected by the planned works. Let us know whether the premises will remain in use and who can arrange access.
