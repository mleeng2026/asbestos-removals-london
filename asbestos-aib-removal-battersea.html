Questions about asbestos AIB removal in Battersea

My Battersea survey confirms asbestos AIB. Can you quote before the refurbishment starts?

Yes. Send the relevant report page and tell us what the refurbishment needs to achieve. We need to distinguish the board identified by the survey from the area your builder actually needs removed: those are not always the same scope.

A ceiling section, a service riser and several rooms can involve different access and handover arrangements. Include a target start date if you have one; you do not need a complete project pack to ask for an asbestos AIB removal quote.

Can asbestos AIB removal be planned in a Battersea flat with shared entrances or lifts?

It may be possible, but the working area and access route need assessment before arrangements are agreed. For a managed flat around Battersea Park Road or Nine Elms, the important question is how the affected room connects to entrances, corridors, lifts and other occupied areas.

Tell us who manages the building and whether a lift or service entrance must be booked. We do not assume every block has the same rules, or that residents can remain beside the work. Our asbestos removal service in Battersea also covers enquiries involving other materials.

What if my builder has found a board but nobody knows whether it is asbestos AIB?

You can contact us before the material is confirmed. Describe where the board is and what work has stopped; avoid further disturbance while its identity and condition are assessed. A photograph may show its position and access, but cannot establish that it is asbestos insulating board.

We can discuss whether targeted sampling or a survey appropriate to the planned refurbishment is needed. The asbestos surveys and testing information explains the assessment route; an existing report is not a condition of making an enquiry.

Does asbestos AIB removal in Battersea always need an HSE-licensed contractor?

Most asbestos AIB removal is licensable, although limited work can fall outside licensing when the relevant conditions are met. Panel size alone does not decide this: the task, condition, likely disturbance and assessment all matter.

You do not need to choose the category yourself. We discuss the confirmed material and proposed work before the route is agreed. For the broader service, see licensed asbestos removal in London. Timing must allow for the requirements of the actual job; an urgent enquiry is not a promise of an immediate start.

What needs to happen before builders return after asbestos AIB removal?

The agreed asbestos work, cleaning and applicable checks must be completed before the affected area is handed back. The handover should make clear which material has been removed, any excluded areas and which documents are being supplied.

Tell us whether rewiring, ceiling replacement or a wider strip-out is next.
