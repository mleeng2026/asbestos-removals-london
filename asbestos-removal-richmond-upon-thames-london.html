Asbestos removal across Richmond upon Thames.

Do you provide asbestos removal across the whole Richmond upon Thames borough?

Yes. We take asbestos removal enquiries across Richmond upon Thames, including Richmond, Twickenham, Teddington, Barnes, Kew, Hampton and surrounding areas. Send the postcode and nearest local area so the enquiry can be matched to the right location and access context.

Is the Richmond town page different from this borough page?

Yes. This page is the borough-wide hub. The Richmond TW9/TW10 page is deliberately narrower and focuses on Richmond town. Twickenham and Teddington also have dedicated pages. We keep the hierarchy clear so the pages do not all compete for the same local intent.

What asbestos removal services do you cover in Richmond upon Thames?

Enquiries can include asbestos AIB removal, asbestos Artex removal, asbestos textured coating removal, asbestos floor tile removal, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, asbestos garage roof removal, asbestos roof removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal and asbestos cement flue removal.

Can you help with asbestos roof removal in Richmond upon Thames?

Yes. For asbestos garage roof removal or larger asbestos roof removal, send the postcode, safe photos and approximate dimensions if available. Tell us whether a roofer, demolition contractor or redevelopment programme is waiting on the asbestos work.

Do asbestos AIB removal and asbestos pipe lagging removal need a licensed contractor?

Many asbestos AIB removal and asbestos pipe lagging removal jobs require an HSE-licensed asbestos contractor, but the confirmed material, condition and work method determine the correct route. You do not need to classify the job yourself.

Do I need an asbestos survey before refurbishment?

If asbestos has already been identified, contact us about removal. If the material is unknown and refurbishment will disturb the building, tell us what work is planned and we can explain whether an asbestos refurbishment survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Can you work with landlords, managing agents and commercial contractors?

Yes. We take enquiries from landlords, property managers, facilities teams, main contractors, developers and business owners. Tell us who occupies the building, which areas are affected and what part of the programme or handover is waiting on the asbestos work.

What should I send for a Richmond upon Thames asbestos quote?

Send the property postcode, nearest local area, what asbestos has been identified and what needs to happen next. If available, include survey pages, sample results, photos, measurements or drawings. You can still contact us without paperwork.
