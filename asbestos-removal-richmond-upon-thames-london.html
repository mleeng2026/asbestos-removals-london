Do you provide asbestos removal across Richmond upon Thames?

What asbestos materials can be removed in Richmond upon Thames?

Borough-wide enquiries can include AIB panels and soffits, pipe insulation and plant-room materials, asbestos cement roofs, commercial building materials, textured coatings, floor tiles and refurbishment-related removal.

Can I ask about asbestos removal in Richmond or Twickenham?

Yes. We regularly deal with jobs where a survey has already identified the asbestos and contractors or property teams need the removal completed before the next stage can begin.

Do I need an asbestos survey before refurbishment in Richmond upon Thames?

If the asbestos has already been identified, send the relevant survey section or removal scope. Where intrusive refurbishment is planned and the material is still unknown, the correct survey may be needed before work proceeds.

Do you handle homes and commercial buildings in Richmond upon Thames?

Yes. We handle enquiries involving period houses, blocks of flats, schools, shops, offices, managed properties and larger buildings across the borough. The removal method depends on the material, condition and planned work.

Does asbestos removal in Richmond upon Thames require a licensed contractor?

It depends on the asbestos material and how it will be removed. AIB and pipe lagging often need an HSE-licensed contractor. Call us and we will explain what applies to your job.

What should I send for a Richmond upon Thames asbestos quote?

For larger borough jobs, send the survey, photographs, drawings or relevant specification where available. For smaller jobs, a clear description and photos are often enough to start the conversation.

Will asbestos waste from Richmond upon Thames be disposed of legally?

The agreed removal scope should include suitable wrapping, lawful transport and disposal at an authorised facility, with the relevant waste documentation.
