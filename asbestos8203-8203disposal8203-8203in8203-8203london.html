FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Disposal London questions.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call us and explain what needs to come out. If you already have a survey or sample result, you can include it, but you do not need paperwork before contacting us.

Can I call before I have paperwork?

Yes. Call us and explain the problem. If the material is not known and planned work will disturb it, sampling or a suitable survey may be needed before the final plan is agreed.

Do you cover all London boroughs?

Yes. We take domestic and commercial asbestos enquiries across all 32 London boroughs and surrounding Greater London areas.

Will the asbestos waste be disposed of legally?

The removal scope should include lawful transport and disposal at an authorised facility, with the relevant waste or consignment documentation for the work.
