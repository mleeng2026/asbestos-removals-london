FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about asbestos surveys in London.

How do I ask for an asbestos survey quote?

Call us and explain the property, the areas you need surveyed and any work you are planning. A real person will tell you what information is useful for the quotation.

Which type of asbestos survey do I need?

A management survey is used for normal occupation and maintenance. A refurbishment or demolition survey is used before work that will disturb the building fabric. Call us if you are unsure.

Can an asbestos survey cover one room or work area?

A refurbishment survey can be planned around the area affected by the proposed work. Tell us exactly what work is planned so the correct area can be covered.

What is included in an asbestos survey report?

The report records the agreed areas inspected, asbestos-containing materials found or presumed, their condition, photographs and any sample results. Areas that could not be accessed should also be recorded.

Do you cover all London boroughs?

Yes. We take domestic and commercial asbestos survey enquiries across all 32 London boroughs and surrounding Greater London areas.

Do I need a survey if asbestos is already confirmed?

Not automatically. If you already know what needs to be removed, call us about asbestos removal. We will tell you if any further information is genuinely needed.
