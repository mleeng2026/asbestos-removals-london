FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Asbestos Removal Services London questions.

What asbestos removal services do you provide in London?

We take enquiries for asbestos AIB removal, asbestos Artex removal, asbestos textured coating removal, asbestos floor tile removal, asbestos bitumen adhesive removal, asbestos garage roof removal, asbestos roof removal, asbestos AIB soffit removal, asbestos cement flue removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal, asbestos insulation removal and asbestos cement sheet removal, plus commercial and licensed work where required.

Do I need an asbestos survey before I contact you?

No. If asbestos has already been identified, contact us about removal. If the material has not been confirmed, tell us where it is and what work you are planning. We can explain whether an asbestos survey, sample or test is the sensible next step.

Which asbestos removal work may need an HSE-licensed contractor?

The confirmed material, condition and work method determine the correct route. Many asbestos AIB removal, asbestos pipe lagging removal and asbestos insulation removal jobs require an HSE-licensed asbestos contractor. You do not need to classify the job yourself.

Can you quote from an asbestos survey?

Yes. Send the relevant survey or asbestos-register pages, the postcode and what needs to happen afterwards. Photos, measurements and drawings can also help, but you do not need a complete tender pack before contacting us.

What if asbestos is holding up builders or refurbishment work?

Tell us what has been identified and which trade or project stage is waiting. Roofing, flooring, rewiring, plastering, boiler replacement, strip-out and refurbishment are common reasons buyers need asbestos removal arranged before work can continue.

Do you handle commercial asbestos removal?

Yes. We take commercial asbestos removal enquiries for shops, offices, warehouses, plant rooms, managed buildings and refurbishment sites. Tell us about occupancy, access, the survey scope and what part of the programme is waiting.

Do you cover asbestos removal across London?

Yes. We cover London and Greater London, including all 32 London boroughs. Send the postcode with the enquiry so the location, access and correct local page can be matched to the job.

What should I send with an asbestos removal enquiry?

Send the postcode and a short description of what needs removing. If available, include survey pages, photos, measurements, a sample result, asbestos register or drawings. You can still contact us if you do not have any paperwork yet.
