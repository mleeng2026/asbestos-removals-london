Plan the asbestos removal around the building and the roofing work.

A warehouse or workshop roof is different from a domestic garage. The quote depends on the height, roof area, access, people working below, adjoining cladding and how the building will stay weatherproof while the roof is replaced.

Business still using the building

REMOVAL AND REPLACEMENT ROOFING

Agree the asbestos and roofing work before either contractor starts.

On a larger building, the asbestos removal and the replacement roof must fit together. The quotation should make clear which sheets are being removed, the order of the work and who is responsible for each stage.

01 / SCOPE

Mark the roof and cladding areas

Plans, roof measurements and clear photographs help identify the exact sheets, elevations and adjoining materials included in the asbestos work.

02 / BUILDING USE

Plan around people and access

Tell us which parts of the building must remain open, what happens below the roof and where access equipment can be positioned.

03 / ROOFING TEAM

Coordinate the replacement roof

If the work is phased, agree the sequence and weatherproofing responsibilities before removal begins. This avoids a gap between the asbestos and roofing scopes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about asbestos cement roofs and sheets.

Do you remove large asbestos cement roofs in London?

Yes. We take enquiries for warehouses, workshops, shops, industrial units, outbuildings and other larger asbestos cement roof areas across London.

What information do you need to quote for a commercial asbestos roof?

Building type, approximate roof or cladding area, height, access, sheet condition, whether the building stays occupied and what replacement work is planned are the most useful starting points.

Can asbestos cement roof removal be phased around an operating building?

Sometimes. It depends on the layout, access, replacement-roof plan and how the building is used. Tell us which areas must remain operational so the programme can be discussed.

Do you remove asbestos cement wall cladding as well as roof sheets?

Yes. We remove asbestos cement wall cladding and flat or profiled sheets as part of agreed removal work.

How do you coordinate with the replacement roofing contractor?

Tell us if a roofing contractor is already appointed. We can agree which sections must be cleared first and when the roofer needs to start.

Can damaged or fire-damaged asbestos cement sheets be removed?

Yes. Broken, badly weathered or fire-damaged sheets need more careful planning than intact sheets. Tell us their condition when you enquire.

Will I receive asbestos waste paperwork?

Yes. Lawful transport and disposal of the agreed asbestos waste is included, with the relevant waste paperwork supplied.

Is this page for a single domestic garage roof?

For a single, double, attached or shared garage, use our dedicated asbestos garage-roof removal page. This page is for larger roof, cladding and commercial cement-sheet jobs.
